# Daily Forge

A simple iPhone-friendly daily accountability app. Check off the work, get the score, and share it with friends.

**Live app:** [https://ironforgecreategm.github.io/daily-forge/](https://ironforgecreategm.github.io/daily-forge/)

## Add it to your iPhone

1. Open the live link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open **Daily Forge** from your home screen — it runs full-screen like an app.

## How it works

- Check off daily items across Body, Spirit, Recovery, Clean, Family, and Sleep. Each has its own point value (max 150).
- Your score ring fills as you go. Labels run from Cold to Forged based on the percentage you hit.
- A day counts toward your streak when you reach the threshold (80% by default). You can change that in Settings.
- Share a score card with friends. The checklist stays off the card unless you turn it on.
- Everything stays on your device (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent to a server. Use Export / Import in Settings to back up or move your data.

Days roll over at local midnight. You can tap a past day in the 7-day strip to review or edit it. The app works offline after the first load.

This is a separate app from [Iron Front](https://ironforgecreategm.github.io/iron-front/).
